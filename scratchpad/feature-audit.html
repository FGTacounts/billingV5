One motion vocabulary for the whole app.Every component had been inventing its own spring — 320/28 in one place, 350/30 in another, 300/30 in a third — so nothing moved quite like anything else. That is the single biggest reason an interface reads as assembled rather than designed. There is now one set of named springs in lib/motion.ts, following Apple's rule: things that enter overshoot very slightly, as though they have mass and settle, and things that leave do not overshoot at all, because a bouncing dismissal reads as indecision.
